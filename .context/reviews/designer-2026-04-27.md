# UI/UX Review

**Reviewer**: designer

## Finding 1: Timeline selector handles — visual indicator smaller than touch target

**File**: `src/components/TimelineSelector.tsx:437-439`  
**Severity**: Low  
**Confidence**: High  

Touch target is 44px (WCAG compliant) but visible handle is only 16x32px. Mouse users may be confused.

**Fix**: Consider adding a subtle hover indicator showing the full 44px touch target area.

---

## Finding 2: Export panel swipe-to-dismiss has no visible affordance

**File**: `src/components/ExportPanel.tsx:111-127`  
**Severity**: Low  
**Confidence**: High  

No visual indicator (drag handle, chevron) that the swipe-to-dismiss gesture is possible.

**Fix**: Add a subtle drag handle at the top of the export panel on mobile viewports.

---

## Finding 3: Dark mode route/trail color contrast may be insufficient

**File**: `src/components/MapView.tsx:38-39`  
**Severity**: Low  
**Confidence**: Medium  

Hardcoded `#06b6d4` (cyan) and `#f97316` (orange) may have insufficient contrast on the dark map style for users with color vision deficiency.

**Fix**: Consider adjusting route/trail colors per map style.

---

## Finding 4: No focus management after track load — timing issue

**File**: `src/app/page.tsx:234-243`  
**Severity**: Low  
**Confidence**: High  

`requestAnimationFrame` may fire before TrackWorkspace has finished rendering. The button query selector may not find the target.

**Fix**: Use a ref callback or `useLayoutEffect` with existence check.

---

## Finding 5: Keyboard help doesn't indicate export-mode shortcuts

**Severity**: Low  
**Confidence**: Medium  

Playback hotkeys are suppressed during export. Keyboard help should indicate which shortcuts are available during export (Escape to cancel).

**Fix**: Add a note in the keyboard help about export-mode shortcuts.

---

## Finding 6: Error boundary reload loses track state

**File**: `src/components/ErrorBoundary.tsx:31`  
**Severity**: Low  
**Confidence**: High  

`window.location.reload()` loses all application state including the loaded track.

**Fix**: Consider persisting the last loaded track in `sessionStorage`. Make "Try Again" the primary action.
