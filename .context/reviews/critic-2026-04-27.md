# Multi-Perspective Critique

**Reviewer**: critic  
**Date**: 2026-04-27

## Finding 1: Scene system gap handling creates ambiguous camera states

**File**: `src/lib/camera.ts:375-427`  
**Severity**: Medium  
**Confidence**: High  

When `globalProgress` falls in a gap between scenes, the camera interpolates with `elapsedSec=0` for rotation-dependent modes, meaning orbit/overview cameras freeze during gaps. For a cinematic video, this looks like a bug.

**Fix**: Consider treating gaps as extensions of the nearest scene, or document the gap behavior clearly in the scene editor UI.

---

## Finding 2: Export memory estimation formula may be too conservative

**File**: `src/lib/videoEncoder.ts:50-66`  
**Severity**: Low  
**Confidence**: Medium  

The 256MB in-memory cap may be too low for modern browsers (2-4GB available) but too high for mobile.

**Fix**: Consider making the cap dependent on `navigator.deviceMemory` or `performance.memory`.

---

## Finding 3: No compile-time check for i18n completeness across locales

**File**: `src/lib/i18n.ts`  
**Severity**: Medium  
**Confidence**: High  

`TranslationKey` is derived from `translations.en`, but there is no check that other locales contain all the same keys. A missing translation would silently fall back to English.

**Fix**: Add a build-time script or vitest test that asserts every key in `translations.en` exists in every other locale.

---

## Finding 4: No per-component error boundaries — one crash kills the whole app

**File**: `src/app/page.tsx`  
**Severity**: Medium  
**Confidence**: High  

The top-level `ErrorBoundary` wrapping `HomeInner` means any component crash shows the error fallback for the entire app.

**Fix**: Add error boundaries around major component subtrees (MapView, TrackWorkspace, ExportPanel).

---

## Finding 5: `usePlaybackController` uses both rAF and setTimeout — potential jitter

**File**: `src/lib/usePlaybackController.ts:111-115`  
**Severity**: Low  
**Confidence**: High  

The dual scheduling pattern (rAF + 250ms setTimeout fallback) is correct but hard to reason about.

**Fix**: Use only `setTimeout` with 16ms interval when `document.hidden`, and rAF when visible.

**File**: `src/app/page.tsx:512`  
**Severity**: Low

## Finding 7: CSP hardening assumes single CSP meta tag

**File**: `scripts/harden-static-export.mjs:110-118`  
**Severity**: Low  
**Confidence**: Medium  

If Next.js ever emits multiple CSP meta tags, only the first would be replaced.

**Fix**: After replacement, verify there is exactly one CSP meta tag in the output.
