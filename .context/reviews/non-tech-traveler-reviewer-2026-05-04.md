# Non-Technical Traveler Review (Mina) — Travelback (2026-05-04)

## Overall Impression

I tried the app from the perspective of a non-technical user. The upload flow is clear, the map animation is cool, and the export process is straightforward. The Korean translations are natural. The import guide covers the apps I'd actually use.

**Grade: B+**

## Flow Walkthrough

### 1. Landing Page
The landing page shows a preview animation that gives me an idea of what the app does. The "Try with a sample trip" button is great — I don't need to find my own file first. The "Browse Files" button is clear. The "Draw a route on the map" option is a nice alternative for planning.

**Issue**: The subtitle says "Turn Google Timeline JSON, GPX, or KML travel logs into a video" — I know what Google Timeline is, but "GPX" and "KML" are jargon. The Korean subtitle is better: "여행을 영상으로 만들어보세요" (Make your trip into a video).

### 2. File Selection
The drag-and-drop area is obvious. The format hint tells me to extract the ZIP first — this is exactly the mistake I'd make. The iOS tip about the Files app is helpful.

**Issue**: No issue. This is well-designed.

### 3. Map + Playback
After uploading, the map shows my route with a pulsing dot. The play button is obvious. The timeline selector at the bottom lets me trim the route.

**Issue**: The playback controls are floating over the map. On a small screen, they might overlap with the route. But this is acceptable for a web app.

### 4. Camera/Scene Editing
The "Camera" button opens a panel with presets like "Cinematic", "Simple", "Bird's Eye", "Dynamic". These names are intuitive. I don't need to understand zoom, pitch, or bearing to use the presets.

**Issue**: The scene editor shows "From %" and "To %" which are a bit technical. But the presets make this optional.

### 5. Export
The export panel is clear: Resolution, Duration, Quality. The preset names like "TikTok / Shorts / Reels" tell me exactly what I need. The estimated time and file size are helpful.

**Issue**: The "Advanced" section shows Codec, FPS, Mbps — these are technical. But they're hidden by default, so I don't need to touch them.

### 6. Post-Export
After export, I see a video preview, a download button, and a share button. The platform-specific tips (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube) are exactly what I need.

**Issue**: No issue. This is great.

### 7. Mobile Experience
The buttons have minimum touch targets. The layout adapts to small screens. The "Load New File" button shows a compact version on mobile.

**Issue**: The export panel might be tight on very small phones (iPhone SE), but the swipe-to-dismiss gesture helps.

### 8. Light/Dark Mode
The app respects my system preference. The theme toggle is in the toolbar. Both modes look good.

**Issue**: No issue.

### 9. Error States
If I upload a photo instead of a GPS file, I get a clear error message with a recovery hint: "That file is not a travel route file. Choose an extracted Google Timeline .json, GPX, or KML file."

**Issue**: No issue. The error message is helpful.

### 10. Language/i18n
The Korean translations are natural and not machine-translated. The Google Guide instructions in Korean are clear. The camera mode names are well-localized.

**Issue**: The emoji in the platform tip ("💡") might not render well on all devices. Minor.

## Priority Recommendations

1. **Change the English subtitle** to be less technical — "Turn your travel data into animated videos" instead of mentioning GPX/KML
2. **The English subtitle already mentions GPX/KML** but the Korean one doesn't — this is actually better for Korean users
3. **No other critical issues** — the app is well-designed for its target audience

## What Works Well

- The sample trip feature lets me try the app immediately
- The import guide covers all the apps I'd use
- The Korean translations are natural
- The export presets match my social media platforms
- The error messages tell me what to do next
- The platform-specific tips after export are exactly what I need
